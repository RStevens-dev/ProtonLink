# Approved Field Lock faces

PKE Scanner is the factory default and migration target for the old numeric face preference. Eleven approved faces are selectable through Settings and Next Face. Subsequent choices persist by stable ID. PKE uses four decorative wandering status contacts confined to their quadrants; offline contacts are gray. A shared alert banner preserves radar visibility during faults; other faces retain full-screen alerts. Analog needle wobble increases with power. Electronic Marquee uses a 5x7 dot matrix, scrolling status and a persisted HSV color wheel, saturation and brightness settings. Amber is default. No extra equipment calls or sensors are required. Canvas designs are adaptations of approved concepts, not exact pixel replicas or native watch firmware. Reduced motion is supported; actual hardware performance is unverified.
